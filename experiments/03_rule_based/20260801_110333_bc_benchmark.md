## Analysis
The BC agent demonstrates an ability to play the game and follow constraints (as learned from the replays), but it has not effectively generalized the concept of "winning" the game by taking 6 prize cards. It performs particularly poorly against completely random actions (`RandomBot`) and aggressive prioritization (`AggroBot`), likely because human play in the dataset rarely exhibits these exact distributions, causing the BC model to drift out of distribution and fail.

This validates the requirement for Phase 2.2: Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) Reinforcement Learning. PPO will explicitly optimize the agent for the dense reward signal (taking prize cards) and the final sparse reward (winning the game).
